# MY TOMORROW — VALORA

## High concept

An offline, third-person 3D life simulator in a fictional English-inspired European town around the year 2000. The player creates a character, finds work, manages money and daily needs, builds a home, meets residents and gradually shapes an independent life.

## Core loop

Explore the town → meet people and discover opportunities → work or complete tasks → earn Valora Crowns (VC) → manage everyday expenses → improve your home and relationships → unlock new activities.

## Six-step production plan

### Step 1 — Playable foundation
- Reliable Godot project and Android export pipeline.
- Small explorable town with clear landmarks.
- Third-person movement, camera and touch controls.
- First objective, interaction feedback and basic save/load.
- Performance baseline for mid-range Android devices.

### Step 2 — Player identity
- Player-created character.
- Appearance choices and a persistent profile.
- Character data safely stored with the save file.

### Step 3 — Home and daily life
- Home access and a meaningful rest routine.
- Food, hunger, energy and a readable in-game clock.
- Furniture and simple home customization.
- Consistent save/load across sessions.

### Step 4 — Work and economy
- Multiple entry-level jobs and understandable shifts.
- Valora Crowns (VC), wages, prices and recurring expenses.
- Shops and useful everyday purchases.
- Economy balancing so progress feels steady rather than grindy.

### Step 5 — Town and relationships
- Residents with schedules and simple daily routines.
- Dialogue, friendship and social activities.
- Quests and local events that connect locations and characters.
- Gradual expansion of the town.

### Step 6 — First complete release
- Visual, audio and interface polish.
- Android performance, memory and battery optimization.
- Full playthrough testing, bug fixing and save compatibility.
- Release candidate only after successful automated export and device testing.

## Visual direction

- Warm, grounded early-2000s English-inspired European town atmosphere.
- Readable pale-blue and pale-green interface with restrained highlights.
- Stylized but proportionate original 3D assets.
- Landscape-first Android presentation.

## Product constraints

- Offline-first; no mandatory account or network connection.
- Free core experience and no hidden paid dependencies.
- Optimize for mid-range Android hardware.
- Keep save data versioned and backward-compatible where practical.
- Do not call an APK playable until it has been built, installed and tested.
